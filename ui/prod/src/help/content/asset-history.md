# Asset History

**Asset History** is the timeline of everything recorded for one asset — every attribute added, changed, or removed, plus alert events. To open it, click the three-dots menu on an asset's row in **Explore** and choose **Asset History**, or click the clock icon on its details page.

## What it shows

- **Time range** — **24h**, **7d**, or **30d** (default 7d). This is the only control that changes what the server returns.
- **Event timeline** — a chart with two lanes, alerts above and attribute changes below, bucketed by day and coloured **Updated**, **Added**, **Removed**, and **Alert**. Click a day's bucket to filter the table to that day; click it again to clear.
- **Events table** — Date, Event, Attribute, Old Value, New Value, and Source. Filter with the *Search events* box or the event-type dropdown (*All / Added / Updated / Removed*), sort by column, and page through 20 at a time. Long values are truncated with a click to expand.
